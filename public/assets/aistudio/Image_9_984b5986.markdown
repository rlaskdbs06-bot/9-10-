## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an approachable, reassuring, and impeccably organized travel companion for first-time visitors to France and Switzerland. Navigating international transit systems, foreign languages, and complex ticketing can be overwhelming; the interface counters this cognitive overload with expansive white space, clear wayfinding cues, and structured, bite-sized itineraries.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Utilitarian Cleanliness** with **Gentle Tactile Hospitality**:
- **Confidence & Guidance**: A disciplined navy structure communicates safety, clarity, and dependable logistics.
- **Optimism & Fresh Air**: Airy sky-blue highlights evoke alpine vistas and open European skies, maintaining an uplifting travel mood.
- **Generous Affordances**: Prominent touch zones, rounded container shapes, and deliberate typography relieve travel friction on the move.

## Colors

The palette relies on high legibility, crisp contrast, and calming atmospheric cool tones suitable for bright outdoor daylight use.

- **Primary (`#1D3557`)**: An authoritative, deep oceanic navy. Applied to critical actions, essential status tags, high-priority headings, and persistent structural anchors.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`)**: A crisp, alpine sky blue. Used for active selection states, key transit identifiers, interactive links, and forward momentum elements.
- **Tertiary (`#E0F2FE`)**: Soft sky tint used as an interactive highlight surface for active filter chips, timetable highlights, and contextual callout badges.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate gray providing calm, balanced metadata labels, subtext, and inactive UI borders.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) ensures optimal ambient contrast.
- **Card & Grouped Containers**: Crisp layered slate tints (`#F8FAFC` base fill, `#F1F5F9` nested segment fill) create subtle distinction without relying on heavy borders.

## Typography

The typography system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** (complemented in code implementations with Pretendard / system gothic for complete CJK glyph coverage). Letterforms are friendly, balanced, and open, ensuring rapid legibility when glancing at transit directions, train platform numbers, and packing checklists on the move.

- **Scale Considerations**: Font sizes skew slightly larger than default desktop ratios to cater to first-time travelers managing baggage and navigating bright stations.
- **Hierarchy Hierarchy**: Station names and primary destinations rely on `headline-md` and `title-md`. Key instructions utilize `body-lg` with `fontWeight: 500` to prevent visual fatigue.
- **Micro-Copy**: Essential labels such as currency codes, platform tags, and time intervals leverage uppercase tracked `label-md` and `label-sm`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a mobile-first, vertical content flow optimized for single-handed thumb operation.

- **Canvas Safe Area & Margins**: Default horizontal screen margins are fixed at `1.25rem` (20px), preserving comfortable breathing room away from screen bezel edges.
- **Rhythm & Grid**: Content sections align to a cohesive 4px baseline rhythm. Interactive elements conform to a primary touch target of at least 48px to 56px in height.
- **Grouping Logic**:
  - Tight groupings (icon to title, chip internal padding): `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
  - Component interior padding (cards, dialogs): `space-md` (16px) to `space-lg` (24px).
  - Vertical section separation (between itinerary days or transit steps): `space-xl` (36px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, dirty drop shadows in favor of a bright, sunlit aesthetic that combines subtle surface tinting with soft ambient diffusion.

- **Surface Level 0 (Base)**: `#FFFFFF` screen backdrop.
- **Surface Level 1 (Default Cards)**: Background fill `#F8FAFC` framed with a fine, translucent border (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated & Floating Elements)**: Floating modals, bottom navigation bars, and active ticket badges use pure `#FFFFFF` resting on a soft ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(29, 53, 87, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(29, 53, 87, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Action Sticky Trays)**: Persistent bottom CTAs utilize a smooth upward gradient scrim fading from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0)` into `#FFFFFF` at 100%.

## Shapes

The shape system conveys a welcoming, friendly, and non-intimidating feel.

- **Base Components (`roundedness: 2`)**: Standard inputs, alert blocks, and compact listing rows use an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius.
- **Content Cards & Panels**: Larger structural cards use `rounded-lg` (16px) to `rounded-xl` (24px) for a soft, pebble-smooth corner profile.
- **Interactive Chips & Action Pills**: Filters, category tags, and primary bottom buttons utilize fully rounded ends (pill / circular radius) for comfortable, friction-free tactile targets.

## Components

### Top Navigation Bar
- **Structure**: Clean 56px height bar on `#FFFFFF` base with a 1px soft border below (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Left Action**: Dedicated `[이전]` (Back) group containing a clean chevron icon paired with an explicit `이전` (Back) text label (`title-md`, `#1D3557`), ensuring zero ambiguity for first-time travelers.
- **Center**: Current page or destination context (e.g., "파리 1일차", "인터라켄 이동").
- **Right Action**: Contextual utilities such as language switch, map toggle, or currency converter quick-launch.

### Primary CTA Buttons
- **Touch Target**: Standard height of 56px (`h-14`) with a full pill radius.
- **Style**: Solid `#1D3557` (Deep Navy) background, pure white bold text (`label-lg`), and zero borders.
- **Active State**: Pressed feedback scales slightly to `98%` with a momentary tint shift to `#0F172A`.

### Interactive Chips
- **Inactive State**: Pill shape, background `#F1F5F9`, text `#64748B` (`label-md`), fine outline in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Active / Selected State**: Pill shape, background `#E0F2FE` (Tertiary sky tint), text `#0284C7` (Secondary blue), fine accent border `#38BDF8`.

### Cards & Timelines
- **Itinerary Card**: Surface fill `#F8FAFC`, radius 20px, internal padding `16px`. Left-accented with transportation badges (e.g., TGV, RER, SBB) rendered in high-contrast miniature pills.
- **Checklist & Tips Card**: White card with subtle cool gray outline, paired with gentle sky-blue status icons for currency advice, plug adapters, and ticket validation warnings.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields**: Height 52px, radius 12px, soft slate fill (`#F8FAFC`), border in `#E2E8F0`. Focus state shifts the border to `#0284C7` with a 3px outer ring in `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 22px touch targets with a distinct `#1D3557` active check state, designed for confident packing and itinerary milestone confirmations.
